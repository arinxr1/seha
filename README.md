# 🏥 Sehah (صحة) - Healthcare Appointment Website

A smart healthcare website designed to streamline clinic and primary healthcare center appointment bookings in advance from home, helping patients skip waiting queues and avoid overcrowding.

## 🎯 Key Features & Services
1. **Beneficiary Organization & Notifications:** Efficiently manage appointments and notify patients about their visits.
2. **Smart Congestion Management:** Suggest the nearest alternative neighborhood health center in case of high traffic or crowding.
3. **Initial Symptom Triage:** Provide an initial assessment of symptoms to determine if a case requires urgent emergency transport or priority doctor consultation.
4. **Online Pre-Registration:** Enable patients to register online from home before visiting the facility.

## 💡 Vision & Inspiration
Inspired by the data-driven and AI solutions championed by organizations like **SDAIA (Saudi Data and AI Authority)** and **[SDAIA Academy](https://github.com/SDAIAAcademy)** to empower the healthcare sector and enhance quality of life under Saudi Vision 2030.
